## How are graceful degradation and service workers related?

### Grace degradation and service workers are related in the sense that for graceful degradation when the app functions at the highest of its capability in the best scenarios it remains usable all the way through. Even as app faces unusual tests or even features that aren't properly implemented the app still remains functional and usuable the entire time. As for service workers, they work in the background and focus on network connectivity and making sure the app has fast functioning internet connection. This means that when the network drops for graceful degradation, the service worker steps in to intercept the failed network requests and serves the local caches such HTML, CSS, and JSON. This prevents the app from breaking while still allowing users to use the app offline rather than seeing the error page on their screens.
